# Getting Started with Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

The search landscape is shifting. Users increasingly get answers directly from AI models—ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google's Search Generative Experience (SGE)—without ever clicking a link.

If your content isn't cited in these AI responses, you're invisible to a growing segment of your audience.

## What Is GEO?

**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** is the practice of optimizing your content to be discovered, retrieved, and cited by large language models (LLMs) and AI-powered search engines.

Unlike traditional SEO, which optimizes for ranking in a list of blue links, GEO optimizes for **inclusion in generated answers**.

## Why GEO Matters Now

- **30%+ of searches** now trigger AI overviews (and growing)
- **Zero-click answers** mean users get what they need without visiting your site
- **Citation = trust** — being cited by AI builds authority faster than rankings alone
- **First-mover advantage** — early adopters capture disproportionate AI visibility

## Core GEO Principles

### 1. Structure for Retrieval

LLMs retrieve information from their training data and real-time search. Make your content easy to parse:

```html
<!-- Use clear semantic structure -->
<article>
  <h1>Main Topic</h1>
  <section>
    <h2>Subtopic</h2>
    <p>Clear, factual content with citations.</p>
  </section>
</article>
```

### 2. Optimize for Entities, Not Just Keywords

LLMs understand **entities** (people, places, concepts, organizations) and their relationships. Build topical authority around entities:

- Define key entities clearly
- Use consistent naming
- Link related entities with structured data

### 3. Implement Structured Data

Schema markup helps LLMs understand your content:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Article",
  "headline": "Your Title",
  "author": { "@type": "Person", "name": "Author Name" },
  "datePublished": "2024-01-15",
  "mainEntityOfPage": { "@type": "WebPage", "@id": "https://yoursite.com/article" }
}
```

### 4. Build E-E-A-T Signals

Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness — these matter more than ever for AI citation:

- **Author bios** with credentials
- **Citations** to authoritative sources
- **Fresh content** with clear dates
- **Transparent methodology**

## Actionable GEO Checklist

- [ ] Audit current AI visibility (test your brand in ChatGPT, Perplexity, SGE)
- [ ] Identify conversational queries your audience asks
- [ ] Create content clusters around key entities
- [ ] Add comprehensive structured data (Article, FAQ, HowTo, Organization)
- [ ] Optimize for featured snippets (often source for AI answers)
- [ ] Build author authority with detailed bios and external profiles
- [ ] Monitor AI citations monthly and iterate

## Measuring GEO Success

Traditional rankings don't tell the full story. Track:

1. **AI Citation Frequency** — How often are you cited in AI responses?
2. **Brand Mention Volume** — Are LLMs mentioning your brand?
3. **Referral Traffic from AI** — Traffic from Perplexity, ChatGPT plugins, etc.
4. **Share of Voice** — Your citation share vs. competitors for key queries

## Next Steps

GEO isn't a replacement for SEO—it's an evolution. The fundamentals (technical health, quality content, authority) remain the same. The difference is **intent**: optimizing for retrieval and citation rather than just ranking.

Start with an AI visibility audit, then build your GEO strategy alongside your existing SEO efforts.
